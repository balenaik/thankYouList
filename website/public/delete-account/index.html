Delete your Thank You List account

You can delete your Thank You List account and all associated data at any time, either from the app or by contacting us by email. The same account is used on Android and iOS.

Option 1

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Thank You List app and log in.
  2. Tap the account icon in the top right corner of the main screen to open the Account screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then confirm.
  4. Enter the email address registered to your account and tap Delete Account.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to balenaik@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request" and include the email address registered to your Thank You List account.

We will delete your account and data within 30 days and let you know by email when it's done. We may contact you to confirm that you are the owner of the account.

Send a deletion request

Data that will be deleted

  • Your account information (name, email address, profile image and sign-in information from Google or Apple)
  • All thank-you entries you have created
  • All positive statements you have created

Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Data that will be kept

We do not keep any data associated with your account after deletion. Anonymous usage statistics collected through Google Analytics for Firebase, which cannot identify you, may be kept according to Google's data retention policy.

Note: Logging out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Settings stored only on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.
